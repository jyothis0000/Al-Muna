How a project runs

  1. Scope

    First you email me about what you have so far, such as your current branding or website, and tell me what you're looking for and the directions we could go. Or we get on a call if that's easier, and I'll ask whatever I need to know. Then I send you a written proposal with the scope and a fixed price for you to approve.

  2. Draft

    After you approve it, you give me your specifics, such as logo files and access to your domain, which stays registered in your name. If you don't have a brand yet, I make a short set of brand guidelines and a homepage mockup of a few sections, possibly in a few variations, for you to approve first. I do all my designing through code from the very start, so when you approve, I build out the full website and the next thing you see is the real site on a private link, rather than a screenshot or a Figma frame.

  3. Live

    Lastly, you go through the website and tell me what to change, and I make the changes. Once you're happy with it, it goes live on your domain, and I keep it running and updated after that through a retainer.

What it costs

Simpler websites start around $1,500, and simple doesn't mean basic. It's still custom designed and built with the same care as a bigger project. A larger site with more pages, more features or a full brand can run several times that, depending on how much there is to build. You pay the first half up front and the second half when the site goes live. For the first month after launch I fix small things for free, and after that it's a monthly retainer. You can cancel it with 30 days' notice, and if you do, I hand the hosting and the code over to you.

Why I do this

Most people judge the validity and value of someone or their brand based on the quality of their website. I care not only about how a page looks but also about how it interacts, moves, and responds. That combination is what makes design. You always notice it in small things, like what happens when you hover something, how fast it loads, and what it looks like on a phone. What I design and build is your digital presentation, and making that as good as it can be matters.
